How It Works

Our 6-step tinting process

From your first WhatsApp message to the final inspection walk-through, here's exactly how SAFA Automotive delivers authorized 3M window tinting, PPF and ceramic coating in Dubai — fast, transparent and hassle-free.

01

Book & Pre-Consultation

Share your vehicle details and preferred 3M film on WhatsApp. We confirm compliance with Dubai's tinting regulations and give you a clear, upfront estimate.

02

On-Site or Drop-Off Assessment

Our certified technicians inspect your windows, check existing seals and walk you through shade options, with a quick quote and turnaround estimate.

03

Film Selection & Confirmation

Choose your 3M film — Crystalline, Color Stable, FX or Ceramic — based on your needs and budget. We explain the warranty and aftercare before you confirm.

04

Preparation & Deep Clean

We meticulously clean the interior glass and window channels to ensure perfect adhesion — removing dust, grease and contaminants.

05

Precision Installation

Using precise templates and installation tools, our certified team applies the film to each window to minimise bubbles and ensure a flawless finish.

06

Final Inspection & Handover

We inspect for alignment, adhesion and optical clarity. You receive warranty paperwork, aftercare instructions and an inspection walk-through.
